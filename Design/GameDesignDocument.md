## 1. Executive Summary & High-Concept Vision
- **Working Title:** REVOLUTION
- **Target Experience:** Ambitious, AAA-style open-world game built with Unreal Engine 5.
- **Core Pillars:**
  1. *Immersive Open-World Exploration:* Seamless world with dynamic weather, environmental storytelling, and scalable fidelity.
  2. *Responsive & Impactful Gameplay:* High-precision character mechanics and player agency.
  3. *Sophisticated Systems & Living AI:* Autonomous NPC behaviors, state-driven encounters, and reactive ecological systems.
  4. *Technical Scalability:* Optimized runtime performance across varying hardware profiles.
